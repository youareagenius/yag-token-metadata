# YAG Tokenomics

The YAG (You Are a Genius) token is designed to fuel the growth of the YAG ecosystem and empower individuals. Below, we outline the tokenomics, including the distribution model, lockup periods, and airdrop rules.

## Token Supply

The total supply of YAG tokens is **300,000,000** tokens (300 million). The token is built on the Solana blockchain, with 9 decimal places.

### Token Distribution

The YAG token distribution has been carefully designed to ensure long-term sustainability and community growth. The distribution is as follows:

- **40% Community & Ecosystem Development**  
  This portion is dedicated to community engagement and ecosystem development. It includes rewards, airdrops, and staking incentives.
  
- **30% Founders & Team**  
  This portion is allocated to the founders and the core team. There is a **1-year lockup period** for this allocation to ensure the team is committed to the long-term success of the project.

- **20% Marketing & Partnerships**  
  This portion is for marketing, partnerships, and business development.  
  - **10 million tokens** are allocated for the 4 team members, and  
  - **10 million tokens** are allocated for airdrops and promotional activities.
  
- **10% Reserve**  
  This portion is for operational costs, reserves, and is distributed equally among the 4 core team members. These tokens can be used for unforeseen expenses or emergencies.

## Lockup Periods

The lockup periods ensure that the founders and team members are incentivized to contribute to the project's long-term success. The lockup periods are as follows:

- **Founder & Team Tokens:** A **1-year lockup period** applies to this portion. After the lockup period ends, the tokens will be gradually released to the team over the course of several months.
  
- **Vesting Schedule:** After the lockup period, a gradual release schedule is in place to prevent sudden price fluctuations and ensure long-term commitment from the team.

## Airdrop Rules

A portion of the tokens will be distributed to the community through airdrops. These airdrops are designed to encourage engagement and attract users to the YAG ecosystem.

The key rules for airdrops are as follows:

- **Eligibility:** Users must complete specific tasks, such as joining the community, following social media accounts, and engaging with the YAG project.
  
- **Airdrop Distribution:** Airdrops will be distributed proportionally based on the user's engagement and completed tasks.
  
- **Transparency:** All airdrop participants will be able to track their progress through the YAG official channels.

## Utility

The YAG token will serve as the utility token within the YAG ecosystem. It can be used for:

- **Accessing exclusive content and events** within the YAG community.
- **Staking to earn rewards** within the ecosystem.
- **Participating in governance** decisions to shape the future of the YAG project.

## Conclusion

The YAG tokenomics have been designed with the goal of creating a sustainable ecosystem that benefits both the community and the project. The careful allocation of tokens and the lockup periods ensure that the project's growth is stable, with a strong focus on long-term value for all participants.
