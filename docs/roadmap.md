# YAG Token Roadmap

The roadmap outlines the key milestones and planned developments for the YAG (You Are a Genius) project. It provides a strategic overview of the project's progress and future goals.

## 2025

### Q2 2025
- **Token Launch**: Official launch of YAG token on Solana blockchain.
- **Community Building**: Engage with the community through social media, airdrops, and events.
- **Partnerships**: Form strategic partnerships to expand YAG's ecosystem.

### Q3 2025
- **DApp Development**: Launch a decentralized application (DApp) that integrates YAG token for utility purposes.
- **Platform Expansion**: Increase the number of platforms that support YAG token for trading and staking.
- **Staking Program**: Introduce staking rewards for YAG holders to encourage long-term commitment.

### Q4 2025
- **DAO Implementation**: Establish a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) to give the community governance rights.
- **NFT Integration**: Launch an NFT collection based on the YAG theme to promote creativity and self-expression.
- **Global Expansion**: Expand marketing efforts to global markets and increase token adoption.

## 2026 and Beyond

- **Mainstream Adoption**: Work towards mainstream adoption of YAG token across various industries.
- **Continuous Ecosystem Growth**: Keep building out new features and partnerships to ensure long-term sustainability.
- **Cross-Chain Integration**: Integrate YAG token with other blockchains for broader use cases and liquidity.

This roadmap is subject to change as the project progresses. Keep an eye on our official channels for the latest updates.

## Conclusion
YAG aims to create a vibrant ecosystem where self-expression and creativity thrive. By following this roadmap, we are committed to building a strong, sustainable project that empowers its community.
